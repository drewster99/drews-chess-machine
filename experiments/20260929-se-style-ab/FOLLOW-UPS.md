# SE style experiment — follow-ups and current conclusions

Written 2026-10-02 by hand; `REPORT-final.md` is generated by `make_final_report.py`
and is not edited. This file collects what the follow-up experiments added. Numbers
are from each experiment's README and tables; the no-ReZero runs are still in progress
(seed 1 at 27k, seed 2 at 20k when written) and this section will be updated when they end.

## Follow-ups

| experiment | change | status |
|---|---|---|
| [`20261001-se-fc1-leaky/`](../20261001-se-fc1-leaky/README.md) | SE FC1 ReLU → leaky ReLU (0.01), from the scale+bias seed-1 fresh net | done, 33k |

## SE: no benefit at this scale, and the scale+bias form costs strength

Seed 1, every 1k mark from 1k to 32k (no-SE minus each SE arm; pElo):

| no SE vs | marks | no SE ahead | mean pElo difference | no SE lower NLL |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| ReLU scale+bias | 32 | 29 | +30.1 | 29 |
| ReLU attenuate-only | 32 | 24 | +15.8 | 28 |
| leaky-FC1 scale+bias | 32 | 22 (1 tied) | +11.4 | 25 |

- Scale+bias SE is worse than no SE (both seeds agree, `REPORT-final.md`); attenuate-only
  and leaky-FC1 are within run-to-run noise of no SE.
- Leaky-FC1 led no SE after the LR trough (25k–32k: +13.5 pElo, ahead at 6 of 8) and led
  its ReLU twin there by +32.7 (9 of 9) — one seed each; +13.5 is inside the seed spread.
- Leaky ReLU removes exactly-dead SE units but does not make them useful: 16–18 of 32
  units per block run below 5% of the block's p90 velocity from 15k to 33k.
- SE adds ~38k parameters (5,208,050 vs 5,170,319) for no measurable gain.

## ReZero (as implemented here): no effect after the first ~3k steps

- Ours is not ReZero as published. α starts at its init 1/√5 = 0.447, which is also the
  tanh cap, so the effective α starts at 0.76 of the cap and saturates at the cap within
  ~1k steps (`20261002-noSE-noReZero/rezero-scale/`): a learned residual scale that ends
  fixed near 0.44. Zero-init ReZero has not been tested.
- Without it the network keeps the residual branch ~2.5× larger (branch scale ≈ 15–17 vs
  ≈ 6.5) and the loss does not care.
- Both no-ReZero seeds trail at 2k–3k and catch up by 3k–5k. After that:
  seed 1, 4k–27k: mean +4.0 pElo, ahead at 12 of 24, lower NLL at 13 of 24 (a coin flip);
  seed 2, 4k–20k: mean +20.9, ahead at 16 of 17, lower NLL at 13 of 17. The two no-ReZero
  seeds differ by about the seed spread, so any effect is smaller than seed noise.

## Limits that apply to all of the above

- One architecture family: 3 blocks × 128 channels, 7×7 convolutions, LayerNorm after every
  residual add (which already normalizes the stream and cancels a common SE β shift).
- ≤ 33k steps: about 20% of one pass over the corpus (`20260624-192615-w3aA5b`, human games),
  one LR cycle shape, one recipe.
- One or two seeds per arm, and minibatches are sampled from the replay buffer with an
  unseeded RNG, so "same init, same feed order" pairs still carry the full run-to-run noise
  (~41 pElo RMS per mark between paired runs, `REPORT-final.md` § Seed noise).
- SE and ReZero are mainly credited with helping deeper towers and longer training;
  neither is tested here.

## Recommendation

For experiments on this network family, use **no SE and no ReZero** as the baseline: equal
strength, fewer parameters, and two fewer numerics hazards (an SE bottleneck with idle
units, a saturating α). Revisit both on a deeper tower (e.g. 10+ blocks), where zero-init
ReZero with a separate cap is the version worth testing.
