Fatconv past 33k, and what the LR cycle does to pElo

lr-schedulearchitecture

Question

The owner asked for fatconv not to stop at 33k. It was resumed on a newer build with --resume-exact. As it continued, pElo fell: has fatconv hit its limit, or is this the LR schedule?

Setup

Run
fatconv continuation, run 20261005-1-1vVl (parent 20261004-15-Pm6B), --resume-exact from the 33k checkpoint on build 2320 (weights, optimizer state, clock, LR cycle and corpus position continued; six gaps accepted with --accept-inexact: rng_sampler, dropout_state, feed_carry, params, lineage, policy_tail); stopped by the owner at 39,093 (SIGINT, clean abort save).
Schedule
LR cycle 0.1 ↔ 0.001, 20,000-step period, cosine in log-LR; momentum follows inversely 0.85 ↔ 0.95; 1,000-step warmup; slow decay envelope (×0.871 per cycle).
Caveat
Build 2320 applies the batch-composition sampler that 2275 ignored; the parameters were set to the nearest uniform equivalent. Probes on 2275 and 2320 agree to every printed digit on the 33k checkpoint.
Measure
pElo and NLL on the --probe-set wide Lichess puzzle probes, every 1,000 steps; both measure the policy only. Seeds of one recipe differ by up to ~25 pElo (R7 and R8: 22.5 over 29–33k).

Interpretation

Most of each cycle's gain comes while the LR falls through roughly 0.08 → 0.004 (21k–27k: fatconv +159, R7/R8 +154). Below about 0.0025 pElo is flat for ~6,000 steps per cycle; at high LR it stalls or dips. At equal LR the falling side is higher than the rising side (18k 1263.4 vs 24k 1316.6 at LR ≈ 0.034), so learning does happen near the peak but is hidden by the noise. Fatconv's drop after 33k (1395.8 → 1330.0 at 39k, then 1352.6 at the 39,093 stop) goes the same way as the first cycle's rise but about twice as far (13k → 19k: −34.1 for fatconv, +6.4 for R7/R8), so it does not show a limit, though the sampler change at the resume is a possible contributor; the continuation never reached the next trough (~51k). Trough-to-trough gain from 9–13k to 29–33k was +134.5 for fatconv against +176.5 for R7/R8.

Takeaway

pElo tracks the LR cycle: it stalls or dips while the LR is high and jumps as it falls, so compare runs trough to trough, never mid-cycle.
