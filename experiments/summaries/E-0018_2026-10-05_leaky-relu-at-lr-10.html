Leaky ReLU everywhere under the 10 ↔ 0.01 LR cycle (arm C-leaky)

lr-scheduleactivationsleaky-relubasic24

Question

Arm C (LR cycle 10 ↔ 0.01) destroyed the net at LR ≈ 3: the policy put 99.7% of its mass on illegal moves and 339 of 1,040 BN-fed channels went dead, frozen at pElo ≈ 585 (E-0017). With per-site activations available, does leaky ReLU everywhere — which keeps a gradient through every channel — let the same schedule survive?

Setup

Net
R7's shape, basic24 input, 3 × [7×7 + 7×7] @128 pre-activation, no SE; leaky ReLU (slope 0.01) at the block group, tower end, policy head, value conv and value FC1 (r7_basic24_leakyall.json, format v9). Start net 20261006-40-7URa: every trainable tensor byte-identical to C's start 20261005-22-yRzB; only the BN running statistics differ (~1%, mint-time calibration through the leaky activations).
Recipe
C's: parameters-C.json (cycle 10 ↔ 0.01, 10k period, momentum following inversely), --seed 20261005 (C's batches), corpus 20260624-192615-w3aA5b, batch 4,096, fp32 policy tail.
Build / run
Frozen DCM-2331-4e70c615-p1headact.app (binary build 2330, sha256 1ae960792717; per-site activations phase 1). Started 2026-10-05 23:13, log dcm_log_20261005-231335.txt; stopped by the owner at step 1,175 (23:43).
Compared with
Arm C (ReLU everywhere), same schedule, seed and trainable start weights.

C-leaky's breakdown went through the value head first: value loss 0.93 at step 300, 6,622 at 350, 32,846 at 400, while policy loss was 7.4, 181, 163. Probes: pElo 497.2 / NLL 4.2782 at step 1,000 and 365.2 / 9.3194 at the step-1,175 abort save (B at step 1,000: 1018.2 / 2.8753). No weight ever went non-finite (0 non-finite values in 102 tensors at steps 1,000 and 1,175), so the trainer's non-finite halt never fired. At the abort save 543 BN-fed channels were past β/|γ| = −3, 177 between −3 and −2, 14 always on; the worst site was the policy pre-BN (93 of 128).

Interpretation

Up to LR 2 the two arms are indistinguishable (loss 5.15 vs 5.14), so leaky ReLU neither helped nor hurt there. At LR 3 both broke. C went quiet: its value channels died, the policy put 99.7% of its probability on illegal moves, and gradients vanished (gNorm 0.02). C-leaky stayed live: with gradient flowing through every channel, the value logits and the loss grew without bound (gNorm up to 393,032) while the weights stayed finite. So the failure at LR ≥ 3 is the step size, not the activation; leaky ReLU only changes its form. One seed; the usable peak lies between 1 (B, healthy) and 3.

Takeaway

Leaky ReLU at every activation does not make a 10-peak LR cycle survivable on R7's shape: the net trained like C to LR 2, then ran away at LR 3 instead of freezing (loss to 4.5M, finite weights); keep the cycle peak at or below 1 on this net.
