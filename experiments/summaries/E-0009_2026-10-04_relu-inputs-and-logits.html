ReLU inputs and pre-softmax logits: fatconv vs R7/R8

numericspolicy-headvalue-headdiagnostics

Question

What is the mean of every value entering every ReLU (anything above 1, or never negative)? What are the min / max / mean of the policy and value logits just before their softmaxes?

Setup

Models
33k checkpoints: fatconv 20261004-fatconv98-b2275-replay-step33000, R7 20261002-bench_v5s3_noSE_noReZero-replay-step33000, R8 …-seed2-replay-step33000.
Positions
8,172 positions from 4,086 games of the training corpus 20260624-192615-w3aA5b, shards 20/26/32/38 (never reached by these 33k runs), two random plies per game, seed 20261004. Repeated on the app audit's 4,097 bot-game positions: broadly the same, except that the illegal-beats-legal row reverses (fatconv yes by 1.11, R7 and R8 never).
Method
fp32 numpy forward pass mirroring ChessNetwork.swift; checked against the app's --analyze-numerics to every printed digit.

Interpretation

No ReLU input is always non-negative, so none acts as a pass-through. The never-positive value FC1 units receive almost no gradient: their optimizer velocity is 0.007–0.21× the layer median, all inside the app audit's low-velocity class (R8 unit 0 is ≈ 0), so they are dormant, close to dead. The policy separates legal from illegal moves by about +16.6–16.7, and the choice among legal moves lives in a ~0.58 spread on top. Near 17, bf16's step is 0.125, which matters only if the final projection runs in bf16 (E-0010). Illegal-beats-legal cases are masked out at play time.

Takeaway

No channel is saturated and only a few value FC1 units are near-dead; legal-move logits sit near +17.3 with a ~0.58 spread, all logits within −18.3…+28.1, value logits within ±4.21.
