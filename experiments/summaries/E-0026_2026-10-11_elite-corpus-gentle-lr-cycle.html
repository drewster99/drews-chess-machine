Elite corpus with a gentle LR cycle (R-elitecyc)

lr-schedulecorpussiluexact-resume

Question

R-replay (ZlrA's settings on the w3aA5b corpus, mean rating ~1,640) levelled off at ~1,610–1,650 wide pElo. Does a stronger teacher, the Lichess Elite corpus (mean ~2,568), lift it? R-elite fed it at a fixed LR of 0.002 and stayed flat (1,628–1,645 for 21k steps), so the owner asked for "a less intense cycle": R-elitecyc branches R-elite's step-21,006 save onto a 0.002–0.02 cycle for the rest of the epoch.

Method

Compared at each run's own trainer step with R-replay and R-elitefresh (both fresh, 0.001–1.0 cycle).

Interpretation

The elite corpus at a fixed 0.002 did not move the network; the same corpus on a 10× cycle gained about 80 pElo over 128k steps and was still climbing slowly at the end of the epoch. One run, one seed, and the cycle is confounded with the extra steps (R-elite stopped at 21k), so the cycle's share is not measured. From scratch on the same 0.001–1.0 schedule, the elite corpus has so far trained slower than w3aA5b (R-elitefresh below R-replay at every 10k step through 80k), so whether the stronger teacher helps only an already-trained network is open.

Takeaway

A gentle 0.002–0.02 LR cycle took R-replay’s network from 1,638 to 1,727 wide pElo on the elite corpus where a fixed 0.002 stayed flat; continue trained networks on the gentle cycle, whose peaks cost ~18 pElo against ~194 for the 0.001–1.0 cycle.
