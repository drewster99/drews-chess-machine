Elite corpus with a gentle LR cycle (R-elitecyc)

lr-schedulecorpussiluexact-resumegpu-fault

Question

R-replay (ZlrA's settings on the w3aA5b corpus, mean rating ~1,640) levelled off at ~1,610–1,650 wide pElo. Does a stronger teacher, the Lichess Elite corpus (mean ~2,568), lift it? R-elite fed it at a fixed LR of 0.002 and stayed flat (1,628–1,645 for 21k steps), so the owner asked for "a less intense cycle": R-elitecyc branches R-elite's step-21,006 save onto a 0.002–0.02 cycle for the rest of the epoch.

R-elitefresh and R-replay: parameters-zlra.json, inverted 1.0 ↔ 0.001 cycle decaying toward step 1M (peak 0.269 by 191,000). Compared at each run's own trainer step.

Interpretation

The elite corpus at a fixed 0.002 did not move the network; the same corpus on a 10× cycle gained about 80 pElo in its partial first pass, about 47 more (best to best) in the second, and about 12 in the first quarter of the third, with NLL falling 2.0654 → 1.9361 → 1.8702 → 1.8653: still rising at the stop, with diminishing returns per pass. One run, one seed; the cycle, the extra steps and the trained starting network are not separated. From scratch on the 0.001–1.0 schedule, the elite corpus trained slower than w3aA5b and, after 2.3× R-replay's steps, only reached R-replay's level (~1,640), 162 pElo below R-elitecyc's final; its BN running-variance ratio climbed for the whole run while R-elitecyc's held. Whether that ratchet costs pElo is not shown.

Takeaway

A gentle 0.002–0.02 LR cycle took R-replay’s network from 1,638 to 1,727 wide pElo in one partial pass of the elite corpus (a fixed 0.002 stayed flat) and to 1,786 at best after two more passes (1,777 at 314,767), while the same corpus from scratch on the 0.001–1.0 cycle peaked at 1,639; continue trained networks on the gentle cycle, and expect diminishing returns per extra pass.
