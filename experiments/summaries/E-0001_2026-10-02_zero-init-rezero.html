Zero-init ReZero (α₀ = 0, cap 1.0)

architecturerezero

Question

Our ReZero had never started at zero: α₀ was 0.447 with the cap tied to it, so it acted as a fixed residual scale. Does the published form (every block starts as the identity, α₀ = 0, cap 1.0) beat the capped form or no ReZero at all?

Setup

Runs
R9 zero-init ReZero, run 20261003-1-NKTv, from 20261002-6-SGuE (derived bit-exactly from R5's fresh net; only the three α tensors differ). Comparators: R5 capped ReZero (se_none seed 1), R7 / R8 no ReZero (20261002-2-5tKN / 20261002-4-T79u).
Architecture
3 × [7×7 + 7×7] @128, no SE, LayerNorm block output, basic30, bf16, 5.17M parameters.
Builds
Trained: R9 on 2290 (9a36f9f, first build reading architecture format v6), R7/R8 on 2275, R5 on 2255. Probed: R9 with 2290, R7/R8 with 2275, R5 by an earlier build (dashboard). Builds read up to ~2.6 pElo apart on one checkpoint.
Training
Corpus replay of 20260624-192615-w3aA5b (the first 20.9M games of the Lichess May 2026 standard rated dump), batch 4,096, LR cycle 0.1 ↔ 0.001 (20,000-step period after a 1,000-step warmup, each peak slightly lower than the last), 33,000-step limit, --policy-tail-precision fp32_from_pre_bn.
Measure
pElo and NLL on the --probe-set wide Lichess puzzle probes, every 1,000 steps; both measure the policy only. Seeds of one recipe differ by up to ~25 pElo (R7 and R8: 22.5 over 29–33k).

Interpretation

Zero-init lagged early (−19.8 against capped ReZero at 5–7k) and finished close to it (−10.4 over the same 29–32k window). Over 29–33k it ended 18.8 below R7 and 41.3 below R8. One seed, and seeds of one recipe differ by up to ~25 pElo, so gaps under that are inside seed noise. The α trajectory per block was not analyzed.

Takeaway

Starting ReZero at zero gains nothing: it ends level with capped ReZero and below both no-ReZero seeds, so the baseline stays without ReZero.
