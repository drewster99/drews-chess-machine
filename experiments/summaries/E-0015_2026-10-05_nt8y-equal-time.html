nt8y vs R7/R8 at matched wall time

architecturespeedhistory

Question

nt8y (1.53M parameters) was only 1278 at 33k against R7/R8's 1504.6, but its steps are cheaper. At equal wall time, which is ahead?

Setup

Runs
nt8y (dashboard CSV, July 2026 build) and Avg(R7,R8) probes.
Speed
Measured alone on this M5 Max, build 2320, fp32 tail, wall time over steps 200–550 of 600 (E-0011): R7 812.1 ms/step, nt8y 443.1 ms/step, ratio 1.833. nt8y's July runs logged ~254 ms per step of GPU time (ms=) and ~335 ms/step of wall time, i.e. about 2.4× R7's wall time here; the July setup (possibly a VM, all-bf16 head) differs and the gap is not explained. With the app's default mixed tail nt8y's 512-channel policy head would likely run somewhat faster (a ratio nearer ~1.9).
Method
nt8y step = R7/R8 step × 1.833. Probe means in windows: ±1–2k for R7/R8, ±2k for nt8y.

Interpretation

nt8y trails at every point except around R7/R8's 20k (where R7/R8 sat at the high-LR part of their cycle). An earlier estimate divided R7's wall time by nt8y's July GPU time (≥3.2×) and concluded the two were level per hour; the measurement here overturns that. This compares two recipes, not just two sizes: nt8y also had SE and ReZero, a July build without logit centering, and (in its later segments; segment 0, which holds every nt8y step used here, is unrecorded) constant LR 0.01 and other weight decay, clip and buffer settings. The 33k R7/R8 window is one-sided (31–33k).

Takeaway

Measured on this Mac, nt8y is only 1.83× faster per step than R7, so at equal wall time it ends about 90 pElo behind R7/R8 at the 33k mark.
