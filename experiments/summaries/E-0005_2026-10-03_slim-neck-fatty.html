Slim-neck fatty: 3×3 stem, wider block

architecturestem

Question

Fatty spends 6.3% of its budget on a 7×7 stem. Shrink the stem to 3×3 and widen the block 216 → 224: is a wider block with a local stem better?

Setup

Runs
R15 slim-neck fatty, 1 × [7×7+7×7] @224 with a 3×3 stem (run 20261004-13-K4Iu, fresh 20261004-12-QsqZ); comparator fatty (E-0004).
Budget
5,153,903 trainable parameters, 321.4M MACs per position.
Build
2275.
Training
Corpus replay of 20260624-192615-w3aA5b (the first 20.9M games of the Lichess May 2026 standard rated dump), batch 4,096, LR cycle 0.1 ↔ 0.001 (20,000-step period after a 1,000-step warmup, each peak slightly lower than the last), 33,000-step limit, --policy-tail-precision fp32_from_pre_bn.
Measure
pElo and NLL on the --probe-set wide Lichess puzzle probes, every 1,000 steps; both measure the policy only. Seeds of one recipe differ by up to ~25 pElo (R7 and R8: 22.5 over 29–33k).

Interpretation

Over 29–33k the two single-block nets are 13.4 pElo apart (3.1 at the 33k probe), inside seed noise; their final NLLs are within 0.002, though slim-neck's NLL is slightly worse over 29–32k. Earlier the order changed: slim-neck led by ~25 at 5–7k and trailed by ~41 at 21–23k. Changing the stem does not close the gap to three blocks.

Takeaway

By 33k, moving fatty's stem budget into width (3×3 stem, 224 channels) made no measurable difference.
