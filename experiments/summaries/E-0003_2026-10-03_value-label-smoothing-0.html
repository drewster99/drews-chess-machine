Interpretation

D trailed early (−14.0) and led late (+13.5); allowing for the ~2.6 probe-build offset, about −16.6 and +10.9. Both are inside seed noise. pElo measures the policy only; the value head's own calibration under ε = 0 was not analyzed, so this says nothing about value accuracy.

Takeaway
