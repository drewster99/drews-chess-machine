Policy label smoothing: per-move vs fixed-total vs none

label-smoothingpolicy-headbasic25

Question

Per-move policy label smoothing (mode 1: a fixed share δ per alternative move, total capped at 0.5) was built on 2026-10-02 and never run. Does it differ from the fixed-total smoothing every run uses (mode 0: ε spread over the legal moves)? With ε 0.1 and δ 0.0033 the two targets are equal at 30 legal moves and differ only away from it (sharper on few-move positions, softer on many-move ones). Arm C, with no policy smoothing, measures how much smoothing matters at all.

Setup

Runs / models
Three arms from one fresh net (20261122-115-Jdx1, init seed 20261122) with --seed 20261122: A mode 0 ε 0.1; B mode 1 δ 0.0033 cap 0.5; C ε 0. Identical batches in all three ([BATCH-HASH] matched at every 100 steps). 6 blocks 3×3 @64, SiLU, no SE, LayerNorm out, basic25, bf16 — 694,767 parameters. To trainer step 40,000.
Build
Steps 0–9,000: build 2687 (d8afa524), replay buffer 1,000,000. Steps 9,000–40,000: every arm resumed exactly from its step-9,000 file on build 2695 (8e889ffd; one training executable, streamed shards), replay buffer 200,000 — the only inexact item (params).
Data
Elite corpus 20260704-215145-op24Gp from game 0, under one epoch. lichess-large (90,378 puzzles) probed at every 1,000 steps; lichess-wide embedded in each checkpoint.
Method
Fixed LR 0.005 after a 1,000-step warmup, momentum 0.9, complement CE on, value smoothing 0.013, batch 4096. Paired differences per checkpoint.

Interpretation

Mode 1 versus mode 0 made no measurable difference here: the mean is a third of a pElo against a 3.5 spread, and the sign flips back and forth. That fits the design: with δ = ε/30 the targets match at 30 legal moves, so only positions far from 30 differ, and on this corpus and model that share was too small to matter. No policy smoothing beat ε 0.1 at all 40 checkpoints, before and after the buffer change, by about 17 pElo and 0.042 NLL — consistent with E-0002, where ε 0.03 finished 20 pElo above ε 0.1 on one seed. Limits: one seed; checkpoints of one run are correlated, so the per-checkpoint spread overstates the certainty somewhat; a small model on fresh, non-repeating data, where smoothing's regularization has little overfitting to prevent; and the puzzle sets score the network's own probabilities, which smoothing deliberately caps. A larger model trained over several epochs could still benefit from some smoothing.

Takeaway

With the arms paired on identical batches, policy label smoothing mode 1 (per move) and mode 0 (fixed total) were indistinguishable over 40,000 steps (+0.3 ± 0.6 pElo), while no policy smoothing beat ε 0.1 at every one of 40 checkpoints (+17 pElo, −0.042 NLL) — drop policy smoothing, or test ε ≤ 0.03, on the next run.
