Question

Does GUI self-play from a fresh init learn on the replay arms' LR cycle (geometric cosine 0.001–1.0, period 10,000, inverted) with a 3-block 9×9 SiLU tower? The owner stopped it at 51,135 steps because the puzzle rating had been flat since about 20k.

Interpretation

The likeliest cause is the LR peak of 1.0, 100× the June runs' flat 0.01, letting the network fit its own replay buffer (positions of one game share its result) rather than learn chess. That fits losses far below every other run and a flat puzzle rating, but it is not shown: the losses are measured on the buffer the network trains on. The same cycle reached about 1,630 pElo on corpus replay (E-0021 / E-0020 arms), and the architecture and self-play temperature also differ from the June runs, so this is not a controlled result. Two follow-ups separate the causes: corpus replay with ZlrA's exact settings, and self-play with LR fixed at 0.01 and momentum at 0.90.

Takeaway

The 0.001–1.0 LR cycle that works on corpus replay left self-play flat at about 540 wide pElo with losses far below every earlier run; do not use it for self-play until the follow-ups (replay with the same settings, self-play at a fixed 0.01) say why.
