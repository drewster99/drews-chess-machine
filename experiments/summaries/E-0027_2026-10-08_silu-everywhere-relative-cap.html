SiLU everywhere with the relative gradient cap (B-siluall)

lr-schedulesilugradient-capexact-resume

Question

B-silu (SiLU tower, leaky-ReLU heads) blew up at the second LR peak at gradient cap 15 (E-0021) and the relative gradient cap fixed that (E-0020, E-0024). The owner asked for "a version with all silu": does a net with SiLU in the policy head, value convolution and value FC1 as well, under the relative cap, train through the 0.001–1.0 LR cycle, and how does it compare with the ReLU and leaky arms? Planned to 40,000 steps, then continued by exact resumes to 100,000 to see whether the cycle keeps paying.

Interpretation

The relative cap removed B-silu's failure: SiLU in the heads too trained through ten LR peaks with no parked channel, where B-silu at cap 15 lost 20 of 128 policy pre-BN channels at its second peak. At the common troughs it is within about 15 pElo of B and the leaky arms after the first cycle (+41 to +54 in the first), which is inside probe noise of tens of pElo with one seed per arm. More steps on the same cycle added at most ~17 (1,647.4 at 58,000 against 1,629.9 at 38,000) and the trough-end plateau then drifted within 1,621–1,640. It does not show whether another seed or a gentler cycle (E-0026) lifts that plateau, or what the 1.0 peak buys on this net; the dips at each peak are about 200 pElo and the run's last step landed on one.

Takeaway

SiLU in the heads as well as the tower, with the relative gradient cap, ran 100,000 steps on the 0.001–1.0 LR cycle with no parked channel or non-finite value and peaked at 1,647 wide pElo (step 58,000), level with ReLU B and the leaky arms; stop such runs at a cycle's trough end, since each LR peak costs ~200 pElo and the trough-end plateau after 40,000 steps is 1,620–1,647.
