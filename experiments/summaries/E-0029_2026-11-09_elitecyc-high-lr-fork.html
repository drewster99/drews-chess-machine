Elite-corpus fork: keep the high-LR cycle vs anneal

lr-scheduleexact-resumebasic24silu

Question

At trainer step 384,964 R-elitecyc left its original learning-rate cycle (geometric cosine 0.002 ↔ 0.02, period 10,000) for a decaying keyframe schedule. A held-out study suggested the high-LR phase was still gaining when it was cut. Was it? A fork continued the original cycle from the same saved state.

Setup

Runs / models
Fork R-elitecyc-hilr (model 20261110-1-lG5l): exact resume of 20261011-zlra-elitecyc-latest-step384964.safetensors under parameters-zlra-elite-gentlecycle.json, 143,563 steps to 528,527 (abort save). Main R-elitecyc from the same file under parameters-zlra-elitecyc-decay.json: peak 0.02 → 0.001 and trough 0.002 → 0.000447 over 386k–486k, then trough → 0.0001 by 586k. Same corpus, batch, momentum 0.90 and feed. 8.27M parameters: 5×5 stem, 3 blocks × 128 channels, 9×9 kernels, SiLU, bf16 (preset v6).
Build
Fork: 2663 (b1ed8b52, adds only a log line). Main: 2620. Evaluations: 2663 (fork, main 520k held-out) and 2658 (main large probe).
Data
Training: elite corpus 20260704-215145-op24Gp. Puzzles: lichess-large, 90,378. Held-out: October 2026 elite games, corpus 20261108-100629-lahw9i, first 20,000 games (1,943,073 positions).
Method
Every 10k checkpoint of both lines (same phase of the 10k cycle) on the large puzzle set; held-out policy NLL at 450k, 500k and 520k.

Findings

The fork trails at every checkpoint from 400k on: 10–17 pElo through 460k, 21–33 pElo from 470k. It gained ~16 pElo over the shared state, nearly all by 450k, then stayed flat; the main line gained ~32 by 450k and held it.

Interpretation

The original cycle had about run its course at 384,964: it paid for roughly another 65k steps, then nothing, while the anneal was worth twice as much over the same span. One seed per line, but the gap is several times the checkpoint noise from 420k on and the held-out NLL agrees in sign at every pair. This does not test a longer high-LR phase that ends in its own anneal, or model capacity.

Takeaway

On R-elitecyc, the 0.002–0.02 cycle was nearly spent by step 384,964: continuing it gained ~16 pElo in 65k steps and then nothing, while annealing gained ~32 — anneal there, and test longer high-LR phases only with an anneal at their end.
