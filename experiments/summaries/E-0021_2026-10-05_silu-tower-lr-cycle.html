SiLU tower with leaky heads on B's LR cycle (arm B-silu)

siluactivationslr-scheduletraining-stabilitybasic24

Question

In ReLU arm B (1.0 ↔ 0.001 LR cycle, E-0017) the tower's blocks.2.bn1 drifted toward the dead line across LR cycles (worst β/|γ| −1.17 → −2.55, 5 channels mostly off by 36k) while constant-LR arm A barely moved. Would SiLU in the tower (blocks and tower end), with leaky ReLU in the heads, keep the tower healthy on the same cycle without losing strength?

Before the blowup B-silu tracked B (mean −3.7 pElo over 1k–19k; ahead through 9k, −41.6 … −54.5 at the first LR peak, best 1571.9 at 19k vs B 1558.5). It blew up at 20,600 (gNorm 2.566, illegal mass 0.80 at 20,650; E-0020). From 21k to 36,066 policy pre-BN stayed at 20 of 128 parked at every checkpoint (21 at 22k); value.bn went 1 → 2 parked from 32k. pElo stalled at 951.9–977.9 over 24k–29k and reached 1250.0 at 36k (B 1620.7). Batch loss came back (3.5684 at 36,050 vs 3.5319 at 19,950) while probe strength did not. The SiLU tower sites have no parked channel at the end, but several have extreme β/|γ| (min −1,562.7 at blocks.1.bn1) and the running-variance max/median reached 9,097 at blocks.1.bn1.

Interpretation

SiLU in the tower is not worse than ReLU while the run is stable, and the tower itself ends without a parked channel where B's blocks.2.bn1 had 5 mostly off. But at cap 15 the second LR peak broke the run, and the damage landed in the policy head's pre-BN layer and stayed there through two LR troughs: parked channels did not come back on their own. E-0020 shows a 1.0 global-norm cap on the same 18k state avoids the blowup, so this arm answers only "SiLU tower at cap 15" (no); a SiLU-vs-ReLU comparison needs the cap on both arms. One seed, one start.

Takeaway

SiLU in the tower (leaky ReLU in the heads) tracked ReLU B to 19k (mean −3.7 pElo), but at gradient cap 15 it blew up at the second LR peak (20,600) and 20 of 128 policy pre-BN channels stayed parked for the remaining 16k steps, ending 1250.0 vs B's 1620.7 at 36k; the SiLU tower itself kept every channel, and the same start with a 1.0 cap avoids the blowup (E-0020), so SiLU towers need the cap.
