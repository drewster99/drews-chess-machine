Relative gradient cap validation: V-1 (per-step ratios on B-silu) and V-3 (fresh start, cap on)

gradient-clippingsilulr-scheduletraining-stabilityexact-resumebasic24

Question

The relative gradient cap (plan RELATIVE_GRADIENT_CAP_PLAN.md) clips each step at min(grad_clip_max_norm, max(floor, k × median of the last N pre-clip norms)) once the window holds W entries. Before it becomes the default it needs two answers. V-1: how far above its own recent median does a healthy step's gradient norm go, and how far did the steps that broke B-silu go — which fixes k. V-3: does the cap, warm-up included, leave healthy early training alone?

Its cap (gCap=) is 15 through step 100 (warm-up), then 7.82 (150), 5.39 (450), 2.77 (1,000), 1.15 (2,000), 0.94 (3,000); the closest any line interval's largest norm came to its line's cap was 5.01 vs 6.48 (interval ending 300). Interpretation

The steps that broke B-silu were 18–48× their trailing median; healthy steps stayed at or below 1.39×. The plan's rule for k (the smallest of 3, 4, 5 with no healthy ratio above it except isolated single steps) gives k = 3, which clips both precursor steps and the whole burst. The blowup was an 11-step burst, not one step: the hard max 15 clipped two of its steps in B-silu and it was not enough. After the burst the damaged network has six steps of 3.0–4.6×; a capped run would not have reached that state, but a k = 3 cap would clip them too. On a healthy fresh start the relative term never bound, so the cap left B's training unchanged to the byte. One start state per run, one seed.

Takeaway

V-1, an exact log-only rerun of B-silu from 18k, logged every step above its trailing median: healthy steps never exceeded 1.39x, while the run was broken by two precursor steps (19,785 at 28x, 19,795 at 18x) and an 11-step burst at 20,599-20,609 (up to 48x, two steps above the hard max 15). By the plan's rule k = 3. V-3, a fresh start on B's recipe with the cap on at k = 3, never clipped on the relative term in 3,000 steps and ended byte-identical to B. Both pass; switching the default from log only to clip (plan P5) waits on the owner.
