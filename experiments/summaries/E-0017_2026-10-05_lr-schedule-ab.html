LR schedule A/B/C: constant 0.01 vs cycles peaking at 1.0 and 10

lr-schedulebasic24

Question

Our 33k tests used an LR cycle (0.1 ↔ 0.001, 20k period), while the long runs that reached 1740–1770 used a constant 0.01 (E-0012). Within a cycle, pElo stalls at high LR and only shows gains on the way down (E-0008). Does the cycle help at all, and can a much higher peak give a faster start?

Setup

Start net
One fresh net, R7's shape with the 24-plane input: basic24 → 7×7 stem → 3 × [7×7 + 7×7] @128, no SE, no ReZero, LayerNorm out; 5,132,687 parameters, ModelID 20261005-22-yRzB, --init-seed 20261005.
Arm A
Constant LR 0.01 after a 1,000-step warmup, momentum 0.9 (cycle off). Stem 20261005-lrA-const01.
Arm B
LR cycle 1.0 ↔ 0.001, 10,000-step period (peaks at 1k, 11k, 21k, 31k; troughs at 6k, 16k, 26k, 36k), momentum following inversely 0.85 ↔ 0.95, decay horizon 10M steps (trough end 1e-5). Stem 20261005-lrB-cyc1. Stopped early only if it blows up within the first 10k.
Arm C
Identical to B but the cycle peaks at 10 and bottoms at 0.01. Build 2323 (the LR-cycle-peak cap raised from 1 to 10; no other training-code change), started 2026-10-05 09:04, when A and B were near step 18,000. Stem 20261005-lrC-cyc10. Stopped at step 513: the policy diverged as its LR passed ~3 (illegal-move mass 0.997 at step 300, then ~0.945; 339 of 1,040 channels dead).
Shared
Build 2320, --policy-tail-precision fp32_from_pre_bn, both --seed 20261005 (identical batches), corpus 20260624-192615-w3aA5b, R7/R8's other settings with the sampler at its nearest uniform equivalent, 36,000-step limit, both on the GPU at once; probes every 1,000 steps. Comparator: Avg(R7,R8) (basic30, other seeds, LR cycle 0.1 ↔ 0.001).
