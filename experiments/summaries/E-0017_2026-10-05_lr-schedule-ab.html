LR schedule A/B/C: constant 0.01 vs cycles peaking at 1.0 and 10

lr-schedulebasic24

Question

Our 33k tests used an LR cycle (0.1 ↔ 0.001, 20k period), while the long runs that reached 1740–1770 used a constant 0.01 (E-0012). Within a cycle, pElo stalls at high LR and only shows gains on the way down (E-0008). Does the cycle help at all, and can a much higher peak give a faster start?

Setup

Start net
One fresh net, R7's shape with the 24-plane input: basic24 → 7×7 stem → 3 × [7×7 + 7×7] @128, no SE, no ReZero, LayerNorm out; 5,132,687 parameters, ModelID 20261005-22-yRzB, --init-seed 20261005.
Arm A
Constant LR 0.01 after a 1,000-step warmup, momentum 0.9 (cycle off). Stem 20261005-lrA-const01.
Arm B
LR cycle 1.0 ↔ 0.001, 10,000-step period (peaks at 1k, 11k, 21k, 31k; troughs at 6k, 16k, 26k, 36k), momentum following inversely 0.85 ↔ 0.95, decay horizon 10M steps (trough end 1e-5). Stem 20261005-lrB-cyc1.
Arm C
Identical to B but the cycle peaks at 10 and bottoms at 0.01. Build 2323 (the LR-cycle-peak cap raised from 1 to 10; no other training-code change), started 09:04 while A and B were near step 18,000. Stopped at step 513, continued by exact resume at the owner's request, stopped by the owner at trainer step 6,116.
Shared
Build 2320, --policy-tail-precision fp32_from_pre_bn, both --seed 20261005 (identical batches), corpus 20260624-192615-w3aA5b, R7/R8's other settings; 36,000-step limit, then (owner) both continued by --resume-exact to trainer step 40,000; A and B shared the GPU throughout. Probes every 1,000 steps (--probe-set wide, policy only). Comparator: Avg(R7,R8) — R7's shape on basic30, other seeds, LR cycle 0.1 ↔ 0.001 with a 20k period (troughs at 11k and 31k), 33k steps.

Interpretation

On this net and batch, a much higher LR peak (1.0, effective step LR/(1−μ) ≈ 6.7 at μ 0.85) works and a peak of 10 does not. Against a constant 0.01, B's advantage is large (≈ 178 pElo best-vs-best; ahead at 39 of 40 probes) and holds over four cycles, though its per-cycle gain shrinks (+172, +28, +23). Against Avg(R7,R8) it is ahead at matched steps (+74 at 33k; B's 26k trough beats R7/R8's best, 1510.6, by 99), but that comparison mixes input encoding (basic24 vs basic30), seeds (seeds of one recipe differ by up to ~25) and cycle period, so it shows the 1.0 peak is at least not worse, not by how much it is better. One seed per arm. B lost 6 of its 16 value-head BN channels early (5 at steps 250–300, a sixth at 1,250–1,300) at its first LR peak; they never recovered, and the run still reached the best probe here, so the policy measure did not suffer visibly — the value head's capacity loss is unmeasured by these probes. B's 40k probe (1507.7) sits on a rising LR (0.5) and is not its level.

Takeaway

On R7's shape a 1.0 ↔ 0.001 LR cycle (10k period) beats a constant 0.01 at almost every probe (best 1632.0 vs 1453.8 by 40k) and beats R7/R8's 0.1 ↔ 0.001 cycle at matched steps, while a 10 ↔ 0.01 cycle destroys the net within 300 steps; read a cycled run at its troughs, not mid-cycle.
