Policy label smoothing ε 0.1 → 0.03

label-smoothingpolicy-headloss

Question

With the shared logit offset no longer able to drift, is ε = 0.1 more policy-target smoothing than needed?

Setup

Runs
R10 seed 1 (run 20261002-5-WkQG), from the same fresh net as baseline R1 (20260929-12-JZOe; R1's trained ID 20260929-22-bWdy); R11 seed 2 (run 20261003-22-oLbF), from baseline R2's fresh net (20260930-1-H1Oq), stopped by the owner at 31,906.
Only variable
policy_label_smoothing_epsilon 0.1 → 0.03.
Architecture
SE scale+bias net: 3 × [7×7 + 7×7] @128, ReZero, LayerNorm out, basic30, bf16.
Builds
Trained: R10/R11 on 2275 (de0f22b); baselines R1 on 2255, R2 on 2259 (no training-math change per the SE report). Probed: R10/R11 with 2275; the baselines by an earlier build that read ~2.6 pElo lower on one checked checkpoint (se_sb 10k).
Training
Corpus replay of 20260624-192615-w3aA5b (the first 20.9M games of the Lichess May 2026 standard rated dump), batch 4,096, LR cycle 0.1 ↔ 0.001 (20,000-step period after a 1,000-step warmup, each peak slightly lower than the last), 33,000-step limit, --policy-tail-precision fp32_from_pre_bn.
Measure
pElo and NLL on the --probe-set wide Lichess puzzle probes, every 1,000 steps; both measure the policy only. Seeds of one recipe differ by up to ~25 pElo (R7 and R8: 22.5 over 29–33k).

Interpretation

Seed 1 was ahead at 28 of 33 probes and ended +20.2 over 29–33k; seed 2 was ahead at 5–7k. Both gaps are under the ~25-pElo seed spread (an estimate from the no-SE R7/R8 pair, imported to this net), and the ~2.6-pElo probe-build offset, measured once on seed 1's baseline and assumed for seed 2, accounts for part of them.

Takeaway

Lower policy smoothing (ε 0.03) finished 20 pElo ahead on one seed — inside seed noise, so not established.
