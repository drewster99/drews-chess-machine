Leaky ReLU at every activation on B's LR cycle (arm B-leakyall)

leaky-reluactivationslr-schedulebasic24

Question

ReLU arm B (1.0 ↔ 0.001 LR cycle, E-0017) lost 6 of 16 value-head BN channels and its tower's blocks.2.bn1 drifted toward the dead line across LR cycles. A leaky value head (E-0019) fixed the value head. Does leaky ReLU at every activation (blocks, tower end, policy head, value conv, value FC1) also keep the tower healthy, and does it change policy strength?

Interpretation

The leaky slope protects the value head exactly as the leaky value head alone does, and nothing else improves. The tower's blocks.2.bn1 drift (β walking toward −3 across LR cycles) continues with a leaky activation, so it is not a ReLU dead-zone effect that a nonzero negative slope prevents. Policy strength sits about one SD below ReLU B at the LR troughs, most clearly in the second cycle. One seed, one start.

Takeaway

Leaky ReLU at every activation kept the value head healthy (value.bn 0 of 16 parked vs B's 6, no zero-velocity value FC1 unit) but did not help the tower or the policy: blocks.2.bn1 still drifted (4 channels mostly off, worst β/|γ| −2.70 vs B −2.53) and low-LR probes averaged −12.5 ± 13.0 pElo vs ReLU B (best 1629.4 vs 1632.0 at 38k), so leaky everywhere adds nothing over a leaky value head.
