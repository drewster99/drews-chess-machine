Tensor health of the single-block nets at 33k

numericsdiagnosticsinput-encoding

Question

Do the final 33k checkpoints of the two single-block nets show dead or stuck-on channels, values large enough to be clipped or to overflow, or anything odd just before the softmaxes?

Setup

Models
fatty 20261003-fatty216-b2275-replay-step33000 (run 20261004-10-YpxP); slim-neck fatty 20261003-fatty224s3-b2275-replay-step33000 (run 20261004-13-K4Iu).
Method
The app's --analyze-numerics audit (build 2320; 4,097 positions: the start position plus 63 games played by DCM's own Lichess bot) plus a per-tensor weight and optimizer-velocity analysis (fatty_tensors.py).

Six of the zero-velocity planes (20, 21, 22, 24, 26, 28) are repetition planes that can never be 1 under chess rules; plane 19 (position seen twice before) fires only rarely in corpus replay and had not fired recently enough to leave velocity. Together they hold about 74k of fatty's parameters (1.5%).

Interpretation

Nothing is close to bf16 or fp16 overflow, and plain ReLU has no upper clip, so large values are not a risk here. The audit's "degraded" verdicts are rounding of small bias values in fp16 (not used in training) and one bf16 bias. The six impossible planes are always zero in training; plane 19 fires rarely in corpus replay, and at play time it can fire where an outside referee decides draws (Lichess bot, --uci), feeding barely trained weights. basic24 removes the six impossible planes, not plane 19.

Takeaway

Fatty and slim-neck fatty have no dead, stuck-on or oversized channels at 33k; the findings are input planes whose stem weights never train and small fp16/bf16 rounding flags on two biases.
